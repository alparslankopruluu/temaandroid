# ScreenMotion
Native iOS theme studio: choose, interact, personalize, save and install.
## Approved product contract
User approved the complete plan on 2026-09-19. UX simplicity and visual craft are the highest priorities. Three tabs: Discover, Library, Settings. No account wall, launch ad or forced onboarding paywall.
22 scenes: 16 original realistic artworks plus 6 in-app pixel worlds (car, motorcycle, helicopter, spaceship, reef, forest). Four coordinated icon packs (24 icons each) and photo/date/calendar/collection widgets. Local drafts and favorites. Gyro, touch and optional score-mode interaction stay inside the app; system wallpaper uses still/Live Photo/video exports and user installation. iOS lock, home and Search backgrounds do not run app gyro or tap physics.
## Delivery
Local working SwiftUI iOS 17+ app and WidgetKit extension, 22 locales. StoreKit local tests and explicitly labeled rewarded-ad simulator. On 2026-09-20 the user expanded the delivery target to production subscriptions through RevenueCat and an App Store 1.0 review submission, subject to payment, signing, device, privacy, metadata, and review-readiness gates. ScreenMotion is a working brand. Market demand and economics remain unvalidated; this is user-authorized product development, not a claimed opportunity score.
## Revenue
Four starter scenes free; all previews free; Pro monthly/yearly full access. Optional rewarded-ad test grants one successful export of a locked scene. Failed export retains its grant. Production RevenueCat integration follows local delivery.
## Acceptance
Meaningful original art, one dominant CTA, at least 44pt targets, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, RTL, Reduce Motion, lifecycle suspension. Same scene renderer for previews/exports. Photos saving and actual lock screen playback are separate evidence. Android is a later separate Kotlin/Compose app.
