# ScreenMotion
Native iOS theme studio: choose, interact, personalize, save and install.
## Approved product contract
User approved the complete plan on 2026-09-19. UX simplicity and visual craft are the highest priorities. Three tabs: Discover, Library, Settings. No account wall, launch ad or forced onboarding paywall.
16 original scenes: 10 vehicles, 2 aquarium, 2 space, 2 nature. Four coordinated icon packs (24 icons each) and photo/date/calendar/collection widgets. Local drafts and favorites. Interactive preview is app-only; system wallpaper uses static/Live Photo exports and user installation.
## Delivery
Local working SwiftUI iOS 17+ app and WidgetKit extension, 22 locales. StoreKit local tests and explicitly labeled rewarded-ad simulator. No production provider setup, remote writes, real ads, real billing, or submission. ScreenMotion is a working brand. Market demand and economics remain unvalidated; this is user-authorized local product development, not a claimed opportunity score.
## Revenue
Four starter scenes free; all previews free; Pro monthly/yearly full access. Optional rewarded-ad test grants one successful export of a locked scene. Failed export retains its grant. Production RevenueCat integration follows local delivery.
## Acceptance
Meaningful original art, one dominant CTA, at least 44pt targets, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, RTL, Reduce Motion, lifecycle suspension. Same scene renderer for previews/exports. Photos saving and actual lock screen playback are separate evidence. Android is a later separate Kotlin/Compose app.
