# ScreenMotion
Native iOS theme studio: choose, interact, personalize, save and install.
## Approved product contract
M5 lets the product team add original realistic or pixel scene ideas through a Firebase-hosted schema-v2 manifest, with localized copy, artwork, optional user-triggered ambient audio, and validated scene-specific animation/particle/light/touch recipes. The iOS editor runs continuous animation, drag/tap response, and device-tilt parallax, while export uses the same deterministic renderer. Bundled assets remain available offline. Remote content is declarative and allowlisted: no downloaded executable code or shaders, user data, or Firestore dependency. Audio is an in-app preview only; it is not part of Live Photo or system wallpaper exports.
User approved the complete plan on 2026-09-19. UX simplicity and visual craft are the highest priorities. Three tabs: Discover, Library, Settings. No account wall, launch ad or forced onboarding paywall.
M4 expands the catalog to 28 scenes: 16 original realistic artworks plus 12 in-app pixel worlds. Four coordinated icon packs (24 icons each) and photo/date/calendar/collection widgets. Local drafts, favorites and per-design setup progress. Gyro, touch and optional score-mode interaction stay inside the app; system wallpaper uses still/Live Photo/video exports and user installation. iOS lock, home and Search backgrounds do not run app gyro or tap physics.
## Delivery
Local working SwiftUI iOS 17+ app and WidgetKit extension, 22 locales. StoreKit local tests and explicitly labeled rewarded-ad simulator. On 2026-09-20 the user expanded the delivery target to production subscriptions through RevenueCat and an App Store 1.0 review submission, subject to payment, signing, device, privacy, metadata, and review-readiness gates. On 2026-09-22 the user approved a separate native Kotlin/Compose Android app with full iOS parity plus technically supported Android/Huawei extensions. On 2026-09-23 the user approved Google Play and RevenueCat Android release work. Internal Testing release `1.0 (1)` is published without assigned testers; Play monthly and yearly subscriptions are active. RevenueCat now has the Play app, both products, `pro` entitlement links and Android products in the current `default` offering. A signed RevenueCat-enabled code-2 AAB is ready, and its replacement upload key becomes valid on 2026-09-25 at 16:14 UTC. The production release remains blocked by that activation time, Play setup declarations/store listing and missing RevenueCat Play service-account credentials. ScreenMotion is a working brand. Market demand and economics remain unvalidated; this is user-authorized product development, not a claimed opportunity score.
## Revenue
Five starter scenes free; all previews free; Pro monthly/yearly full access. Optional debug-only rewarded-ad test grants one successful export of a locked scene. Failed export retains its grant. Production purchases use RevenueCat with store-specific products.
## Acceptance
Meaningful original art, one dominant CTA, accessible targets, scalable type, screen-reader semantics, RTL, reduced motion and lifecycle suspension. Each platform shares one deterministic scene renderer across its preview/export surfaces. Photos/MediaStore saves and actual wallpaper playback are separate evidence. Android additionally supports a real live wallpaper where the OS permits; Huawei-specific delivery is capability-gated and HarmonyOS 5+ native delivery remains a separate project.
